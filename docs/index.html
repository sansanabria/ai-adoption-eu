Who's actually using AI at work in Europe?

More than half of Europeans have tried AI, but only 27% use it for work, and those who do are convinced: 91% say they finish tasks faster. So which countries, company sizes and job functions are pulling ahead, and who is being left behind? Drawn from Eurostat's ICT usage survey and the European Commission's Spring 2026 Economic Forecast.

  • 1 in 5 EU companies now uses AI, up from 1 in 13 in 2021
  • Where you work matters: 42% in Denmark, 5% in Romania
  • Big companies are over 3x as likely to use AI as small ones

The hours saved are similar. The job worries are not.

Monthly hours saved from AI use, by occupation group. EC Spring 2026 Forecast, n=21,207 across 18 member states and 4 candidate countries.

Managers & professionals

8.5hrs / month

Lower displacement concern

Highest work-related AI adoption of any group, and the largest share reporting a major productivity gain.

Plant & machine operators, elementary occupations

8.3hrs / month

Highest displacement concern

Time savings nearly match managerial roles, but this group is the most worried about AI replacing their job.

Students & unpaid workers

9.0hrs / month

High displacement concern

Largest time savings of any group, used mainly for study and job hunting rather than paid work. 41% report a quality improvement, 18% report none.
